# fitness-tracker-app
I have always tracked my workouts in my notes app and have had a severe problem with accessibility of historical data, and visual availability of whether or not I am hitting my goals. The intent for this app is to allow me to have readily available access to relevant historical data while I am working out, but also to provide me with a widget overview that allows me to constantly track the progress of my goals. This app is built custom for my own use and I am currently the only person it is intended for. 

Eventual Features:
- Workout logging
- Exercise history
- Previous-performance lookup
- Set/rep/weight tracking
- Workout history organized by month/year
- Rolling 7-day fitness goals
- Home Screen widget
- Automatic gym-goal tracking from completed workouts
- Diet-goal tracking
- Progress visualization
